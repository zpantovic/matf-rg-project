### Yoda Island Scene

mi20227 - Zaga Pantović

A 3D scene showing Yoda standing on a small island, slowly rotating while a spaceship hovers above him.
The scene combines a peaceful island setting with a touch of space adventure.

## Controls

W -> Move forward  
S -> Move backward  
A -> Move left  
D -> Move right  

ESC -> Close program  
F2 -> GUI Controller  
B -> Toggle Bloom
